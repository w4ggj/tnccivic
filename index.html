Our History

A Community Built to Last

The original Town 'N Country Park Community opened on September 18, 1959, just north of Memorial Highway. Envisioned by builders Charles LaMonte, Mandell Shimberg, Jr., and James H. Shimberg, the development began with 50 homes on Brookside Lane and Murray Hill Drive — priced at $9,200 with monthly payments of just $52.

Marketed as “The Community of 1970,” the neighborhood grew rapidly. Starting with 500 homes south of Hillsborough Avenue, a swim club, Little League fields, and school sites were all donated and built in the early years. By the time the last home was built in 1983, Town 'N Country spanned over 2,000 acres with more than 6,000 single-family homes and townhouses stretching from Memorial Highway to Waters Avenue.

Today, over 20,000 people call the original Town 'N Country area home. Thanks to the dedication of its residents, it remains a vital and well-recognized part of Hillsborough County.

About the Association

What We Do

The Town 'N Country Park Civic Association is a volunteer-run neighborhood organization working to improve quality of life, communicate local government decisions, and build lasting community bonds. We host regular public meetings open to all residents and take positions on zoning, development, and county policy matters that affect our neighborhood.

All residents of Town 'N Country Park are welcome — no dues required to attend or participate.
